# AI Radar

## Short description

AI Radar helps researchers and students discover recent research and understand papers outside their own expertise. Users search an official arXiv category to get a concise overview of up to five recent papers, with plain-language explanations and concrete illustrations. They can then select papers or upload PDFs for single-paper or joint reading, exploring methods, results, limitations, and source excerpts with PDF page references. The interface supports English and Chinese, and reports support English, Chinese, or both.

Building AI Radar taught us how to describe requirements precisely and understand the software development process. Our biggest challenge was turning a vague idea into a clear plan and breaking a large feature into smaller, manageable parts. We learned to move from an idea to defined requirements, then to implementation, testing, and refinement.
