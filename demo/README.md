# English product demo

Duration: 2 minutes 36 seconds. 1080p, 24 fps, English interface and reports, English captions and synthetic English narration.

The video uses edited views of the running local application and real previously generated reports. It shows category search, a five-paper cs.AI overview, paper selection, ready material controls, and a separate two-paper joint reading example. The switch is identified in the narration and captions. It is not a claim of fresh live model generation.

The overview includes arXiv:2610.13889 (RATED). The joint example uses arXiv:2610.06851 and arXiv:2610.06846, totaling 75 pages. Source PDFs and private reports are excluded from the repository.

AI-Radar-demo-en.srt contains the complete English narration as subtitles. narration-en.md is the editable script. The final learning statement is based on the team reflection provided on 2026-10-18.
