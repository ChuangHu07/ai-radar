# AI Radar — English demo narration

## AI Radar

AI Radar helps researchers and students discover recent papers and understand unfamiliar research. This walkthrough uses real, previously generated reports.

## Search an official category

Start with an official arXiv category. A specific code, such as C S dot A I, searches the artificial intelligence subcategory.

## Explore a five-paper overview

Here is a saved overview of five papers. The opening summary explains the shared context before you explore each contribution.

## Make the problem concrete

Each paper combines a plain language explanation with a concrete illustration. This example explains how an agent can avoid tools that stop working.

## Read beyond the headline

Expand the card to inspect the problem, key idea, results, significance, and limitations. Experimental conditions stay attached to the reported result.

## Check where a claim came from

Source excerpts sit beside the explanation. You can inspect the quoted text and its physical PDF page reference instead of trusting a summary alone.

## Open deep reading from the top

The top level deep reading button opens one compact list. Select one paper for a close reading, or several for joint analysis.

## Choose your own reading set

Here we select the RATED paper. The selection remains editable after entering the reading workspace.

## Review materials before generating

The paper arrives with its full text ready. Users can add their own PDFs, replace a source, or remove a paper before explicitly generating a report.

## A separate saved joint-reading example

Now we switch to a separate, previously generated English joint reading example. It compares two papers, totaling seventy five pages.

## Find the shared research question

The joint report first identifies the shared research question. Each paper keeps its own title, source, and evidence references.

## Compare research routes

Next, compare how the researchers investigated their questions, including their interventions, monitoring methods, and evaluation strategies.

## Compare results with context

The results section keeps evidence attached to individual papers. Different task domains mean their accuracy numbers should not become a simple ranking.

## Inspect the original evidence

Expanding a citation reveals the original excerpt, the paper it belongs to, and the PDF page. The source PDF is one click away.

## Keep boundaries visible

The report also explains connections and limitations. Automated checks help trace the sources, but readers still need to verify scientific claims.

## Turn reading into understanding

Finally, joint takeaways connect what can be learned across the papers, while keeping the limits of those connections visible.

## What we learned

Our biggest lesson was turning a vague idea into clear requirements, then breaking a large feature into smaller tasks. AI Radar connects discovery, understanding, and source checking.
