# AI Radar - Interaction walkthrough

Here is AI Radar in action, using previously generated reports.

Start with a saved frontier overview. The top-level deep-reading button opens a compact paper list. Select one paper, or add another for joint reading. Opening the collection brings those papers and their PDFs into the reading workspace.

The collection stays editable. Removing one paper changes the workspace from joint reading to single-paper reading. New generation starts only when you press Generate report.

Now we switch to a separate saved two-paper report, not a newly generated result.

This report compares two papers, keeping their identities and page counts visible. Follow a citation, and its original excerpt now opens immediately. The paper title and physical PDF page stay attached to the evidence. The source PDF remains available for checking important claims.
