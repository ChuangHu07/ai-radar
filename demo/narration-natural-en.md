# AI Radar — natural English narration

## Why we built AI Radar

We built AI Radar because finding a paper and understanding it are two different challenges. Researchers need to keep up, while students entering a new field can feel lost in technical language. We wanted to make that first step easier.

## A clear place to start

The homepage keeps things simple: a central search box, saved category shortcuts, and monthly submission rankings. Deep reading and language settings are available at the top.

## Find your research field

Search an official arXiv category by name or code. For example, C S dot A I takes us straight to artificial intelligence.

## See the bigger picture

Selecting a category retrieves up to five recent papers and generates an overview. Here, we're opening a saved report, starting with the shared research context.

## Understand the problem first

Each paper pairs a plain language explanation with a concrete illustration. This example shows an agent learning to avoid tools that have stopped working.

## Go deeper when you want to

Expand a card to explore the problem, approach, results, and limitations. The illustrations explain concepts; they aren't experimental results.

## Choose what deserves a closer look

The deep reading button opens a compact paper list. Choose one paper for individual reading, or several for joint analysis. You decide what belongs in the collection.

## Keep control of the sources

In the reading workspace, you can upload your own PDFs, replace a source, or remove a paper. Resolve any failed material, then click Generate Report.

## Read papers together

Now we're switching to a separate, previously generated English joint report. This example brings together two papers covering seventy five pages.

## Compare the approaches

The report connects their research questions and compares methods, results, and limitations, while keeping each paper's identity and experimental context visible.

## Follow a claim back to its source

Click a citation to see the original excerpt, its paper, and its PDF page. Automated checks help trace the evidence, but important scientific claims still need your judgment.

## Choose how you read

The interface supports English and Chinese. Reports can be English, Chinese, or bilingual, with interface and report languages selected independently.

## What we learned

Our biggest lesson was turning a vague idea into clear requirements, then breaking a large feature into manageable tasks. We learned through planning, building, testing, and feedback.

## Why this project deserves recognition

We believe AI Radar deserves recognition because it connects discovery, understandable explanations, and traceable evidence in one workflow, helping people approach unfamiliar research with greater confidence.

## A path toward a useful product

With user validation, this could grow into a product with saved libraries, topic updates, and shared team workspaces. A free discovery tier and paid reading tools are possibilities to explore. Our goal is to turn more papers into useful knowledge.
