# Submission Language Standard

Confirmed by the project owner on October 25, 2026.

- English is the authoritative language for hackathon submission documents, including the project description, planning documents, testing instructions, evaluation records and delivery notes.
- The demonstration must have English narration or an English translation and English captions.
- Any non-English evidence included in the submission must have a clearly linked English translation. Label translations accurately and retain the original evidence when appropriate.
- Chinese communication with the owner does not change this submission standard.
- Bilingual application interfaces and Chinese, English or bilingual generated reports are product features; they do not replace English submission documentation.
- Check language consistency before publishing or updating the submission.
