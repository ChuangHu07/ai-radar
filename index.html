What it does and who it is for

AI Radar helps researchers and students discover recent arXiv papers and understand research outside their own expertise. Search an official category to explore a concise overview of up to five papers, then select papers or upload PDFs for single-paper or joint reading. Explanations connect methods, results and limitations to source excerpts and PDF page references.

What we learned

We learned how to describe requirements precisely and understand the software development process. Our biggest challenge was turning a vague idea into a clear plan and breaking a large feature into smaller, manageable parts.
